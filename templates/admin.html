Set RR_ADMIN_TOKEN before starting the server to choose this yourself. If you didn't, one was generated and written to the server's log at startup. You can also open this page as /admin#token=… to sign in on arrival — it is cleared from the address bar immediately, though it stays in that browser's history.

Verified — the other operator's log contains the same contact. NIL — they submitted a log and this contact is not in it, which is the classic sign of a busted callsign or an invented contact. Unmatched — they never submitted a log, so there is nothing to check against. Most contacts at a collegiate event are unmatched and that is entirely normal; only NIL is evidence of anything.

Voiding removes a contact from every total it fed, and survives a restart. It is reversible.
